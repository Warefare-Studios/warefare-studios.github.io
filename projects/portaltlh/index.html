Portal: The Last Hour


Currently, our main project is developing the Portal 2 mod "Portal: The Last Hour".

Taking place 5 years after the events of the co-op campaign, you find yourself being sent over to the Aperture Science Enrichment Center with one goal: Destroy GLaDOS. You are a high-ranking military official, equipped with a weapon (yes, portal with a gun is real) and good luck from your commander. Navigate through the wrecked topside of Aperture, descend into the technological rats nest of clean aperture, find your way into GLaDOS' chamber and kill her once and for all. But will it be as easy as it sounds? And what will happen once she's truly gone?
